Frequently Asked Questions

What website services do you offer?

Wildsite offers brand and website design, copywriting, SEO and AIO strategy, email marketing, and AI consulting.

Do I still need a website in 2026?

Yes, a website is your company’s brand authority. It’s imperative to own your brand—the visuals, the copy, the essence and be the source of truth. It acts as the channel to feed AI information and it needs to be structured strategically to do so.

What makes you different?

Wildsite delivers authentic websites that are optimized for AI in 2026. We use AI in our design and testing process, but it is not our anchor. You and your company are the anchor and we strive to elevate that on-screen, using AI tools and strategy.

I run a restaurant, salon, or studio. Is Wildsite for me?

Yes! We love creating websites for restaurants, wineries, spas, salons, floral studios, therapy offices, and the small businesses that help people celebrate and live well.

How can Wildsite make my website better?

Wildsite is eager to help you make your website better. First, we consider the site structure. Are basic SEO and AI optimization practices (GEO) being used? We then consider the design, the images and the brand—where is it strong, where is it weak? Finally, we factor in technology. How are you capturing leads, are your calls-to-action clear? We look at a range of things to consider how to improve your site.

What’s your pricing model?

We offer three website packages: Simple ($2,200) for a one-to-three page site, Standard ($4,200), and Premium ($6,200+). We also offer email marketing, website maintenance, and AI consulting.

What’s it like to work with you?

We are curious and want to get to know you and your company. It’s a fun process to uncover the layers of your brand, and elevate it on-screen. We love the chance to meet in person, but happy to work with you online as well.

Do you offer anything smaller than a full website?

Yes. Design by the hour covers logos, graphics, print pieces and web edits in blocks of 3, 6 or 12 hours.
